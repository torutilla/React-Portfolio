## Overview

A branding and promotional design project created for Busy Kitchen, a home-based food business.

The project focused on creating a visual identity that communicates the warmth and familiarity of homemade food while remaining eye-catching and suitable for social media marketing.

## Design Brief

The goal was to create a logo that captured the feeling of home cooking and a welcoming kitchen environment.

The branding needed to communicate three main ideas: homemade food, freshness, and a friendly family-oriented atmosphere.

Alongside the logo, I also created promotional materials for the business, including Facebook menu posts and a Facebook cover image.

## Design Process

The design process focused on combining food-related visual elements with a warm and approachable identity.

The logo was developed around a chef's hat, handwritten-style typography, and wheat elements to reinforce the idea of cooking and food preparation. Supporting promotional graphics were then designed around the established visual identity.

Food photography was incorporated into the menu graphics to showcase the products while maintaining consistency with the brand's color palette and visual style.

## Design Decisions

The visual identity uses warm brown and yellow tones to create a cozy, home-cooked feel.

The handwritten "Busy" lettering gives the logo a more personal and approachable character, while the stronger "KITCHEN" typography provides structure and readability.

The chef's hat reinforces the connection to cooking, while the wheat elements help communicate food and freshness.

For the promotional materials, large product photographs and bold pricing were used to make the menu information immediately noticeable in a social media feed.

## Final Output

The final branding package included:

- Busy Kitchen logo
- Facebook menu graphics
- Facebook cover photo

The resulting visual identity was designed to give Busy Kitchen a recognizable and consistent presence across its social media materials.
