## Overview

A commissioned brochure design for SVD Laudato Si' Farm, created to showcase the farm's facilities, environmental initiatives, programs, and sustainable practices.

The brochure was designed to present the farm as both environmentally conscious and professionally managed, while highlighting its natural surroundings and various sustainability efforts.

## Design Brief

The client wanted a brochure that would showcase the farm's amenities and sustainability initiatives while maintaining a luxurious and elegant visual presentation.

The design needed to balance two contrasting qualities: the natural and environmental character of the farm, and a more refined, premium appearance that would make the brochure visually appealing to visitors and stakeholders.

## Design Process

The brochure was structured around the farm's different sustainability initiatives, including sustainable farming, water management, environmental education, energy, livelihood, and lifestyle.

Photography was used extensively to showcase the farm's facilities, activities, landscapes, and sustainability practices.

I developed a visual layout using curved shapes, layered image frames, and structured content sections to organize the large amount of information while maintaining a consistent visual style throughout the brochure.

## Design Decisions

Green and teal tones were used as the primary colors to reinforce the farm's connection to nature, sustainability, and the environment.

Gold accents and borders were incorporated to introduce a more elegant and premium appearance, reflecting the client's preference for a luxurious design.

Curved layouts and circular image frames were used to soften the overall presentation and create visual movement between sections. These elements also helped integrate photographs into the brochure without relying on a conventional grid-based layout.

Typography was structured using contrasting type treatments to distinguish major sustainability themes from their supporting information and maintain a clear visual hierarchy.

## Final Output

The completed design combines environmental imagery with an elegant visual treatment, creating a brochure that communicates both the farm's sustainability-focused identity and its premium visitor experience.
