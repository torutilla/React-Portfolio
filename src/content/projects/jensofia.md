## Overview

Jensofia is an ongoing branding and graphic design project for a local perfumery brand. Since 2023, I have worked on various visual materials for the business, including its logo, perfume labels, product catalogs, promotional flyers, and social media content.

Because the brand is centered around perfumes and fragrance products, the visual identity was designed to communicate elegance, femininity, and a premium feel while remaining recognizable across different marketing materials.

This project is also an example of maintaining and developing a visual identity over time rather than creating a single standalone design.

## Design Brief

The goal was to establish a recognizable visual identity for Jensofia that could work across both physical and digital applications.

The primary direction was to create a logo that immediately felt connected to the perfume industry without relying on overly literal imagery. The identity needed to feel elegant and refined while remaining simple enough to be used on perfume bottles, labels, promotional materials, and social media.

The brand also required supporting visual materials such as product catalogs, perfume labels, flyers, and Facebook posts.

## Design Process

### Logo Concept

The logo began with the initials **J** and **S**, which were developed into a monogram.

Rather than simply combining the two letters, I shaped the forms to mimic the silhouette and proportions of a perfume bottle. This allowed the initials to function both as typography and as a visual representation of the product itself.

The resulting mark gives the identity a subtle connection to perfumery without explicitly drawing a conventional perfume bottle.

### Visual Development

After establishing the logo, I developed supporting typography and visual treatments around the identity.

The wordmark uses an elegant script style to reinforce the premium and feminine character of the brand, while the supporting **"EAU DE TOILETTE"** text provides a more structured typographic contrast.

Gold tones were incorporated into the visual identity to reinforce the luxury and premium qualities commonly associated with fragrance products.

### Brand Applications

The identity was then adapted into different materials used by the business, including:

- Perfume labels
- Product catalogs
- Promotional flyers
- Facebook posts
- Product advertisements
- Other marketing materials

Working across these different formats required maintaining the core identity while adapting the layout and visual hierarchy to each medium.

## Design Decisions

### Perfume Bottle-Inspired Monogram

The central design decision was to make the **J + S monogram resemble a perfume bottle**.

This gives the logo multiple layers of meaning: the letters identify the brand, while the overall silhouette references the product category.

### Elegant Typography

The combination of script and clean supporting typography was chosen to create a balance between elegance and readability.

The script wordmark gives Jensofia a more personal and sophisticated character, while the secondary typography keeps product information clear.

### Gold and Dark Tones

Gold was used to communicate luxury and premium quality, while darker backgrounds provide contrast and allow the gold elements to stand out.

This visual treatment also gives the brand flexibility when creating promotional materials with different product photography and backgrounds.

### Consistent Brand Identity

Because Jensofia is an ongoing project, the focus has not only been on creating individual graphics but also on keeping the visual identity recognizable across different materials.

The same core elements—logo, typography, color treatment, and overall premium aesthetic—can be carried across labels, catalogs, flyers, and social media content.

## Final Output

The Jensofia project developed into an ongoing collection of branding and marketing materials for the perfumery business.

The primary outputs include:

- **Jensofia logo and monogram**
- **Perfume product labels**
- **Product catalogs**
- **Promotional flyers**
- **Facebook/social media graphics**
- **Product advertisements**

Unlike a one-time branding project, Jensofia continues to evolve as the business develops, making it an ongoing example of applying and maintaining a visual identity across real-world marketing materials.
