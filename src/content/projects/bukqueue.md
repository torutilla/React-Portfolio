## Overview

Bukqueue is a mobile booking application developed to improve local transportation services in Tagaytay City. The application was designed for commuters who rely on tricycles and Bukyo transportation, providing a more convenient and transparent way to arrange rides.

The project was developed as a capstone project with the goal of addressing common transportation concerns such as limited availability, unclear fares, difficult navigation, and inefficient communication between commuters and drivers.

## Problem

Commuters in Tagaytay, particularly students and workers, often rely on public transportation with limited schedules and coverage. Traditional tricycle booking can also involve uncertainty regarding fares, availability, and the legitimacy of operators.

The project aimed to provide a more accessible way for commuters to request transportation while giving drivers additional opportunities to receive passengers.

## Features

- Mobile ride booking
- Real-time fare estimation
- Google Maps integration
- Location-based ride requests
- User registration and authentication
- Driver and passenger information
- Route visualization

## Technical Implementation

The application was developed using Flutter and Dart for the mobile interface and Firebase for backend services and data management.

Google Maps APIs were integrated to provide location-based functionality, including map visualization, route information, and transportation-related features.

The application was designed around two primary users: commuters who book rides and drivers who accept and manage ride requests.

## Design Decisions

The interface was designed to keep the booking process simple and accessible. Important information such as pickup locations, destinations, estimated fares, and ride details were prioritized to reduce confusion during the booking process.

Maps and location information were also integrated directly into the booking experience to make it easier for users to identify pickup and destination points.

## Challenges

One of the main challenges was integrating location-based services into the booking workflow while keeping the interface simple for users.

Another challenge was implementing fare estimation and ensuring that transportation information was presented clearly enough to promote transparency between commuters and drivers.

## Outcome

The application was evaluated by its intended users and received an overall rating of "Excellent."

The results indicated that the application could improve the convenience of booking local transportation while providing commuters with clearer fare information and navigation.
