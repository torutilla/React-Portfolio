## Overview

A logo and branding project for Meraki Tattoo Services.

The goal was to create a distinctive visual identity that connected the meaning behind the name "Meraki" with the artistic and transformative nature of tattooing.

## Design Brief

The logo needed to represent a tattoo service while avoiding overly literal or generic tattoo imagery.

The concept was built around a phoenix, a symbol associated with transformation, rebirth, and renewal. This provided a strong visual foundation for expressing the artistic and personal nature of tattooing.

## Design Process

The logo was developed as a single, unified phoenix symbol with several ideas integrated into its structure.

The phoenix serves as the primary form of the logo, representing transformation, rebirth, and personal expression.

The wing of the phoenix was shaped to subtly resemble the letter "M", creating a visual connection to the name "Meraki" without directly placing a letter inside the symbol.

The internal negative space was then used to form the shape of an ink droplet, establishing a connection to tattoo artistry while keeping the overall mark visually clean.

Rather than presenting the phoenix, letter, and ink droplet as separate elements, the concepts were incorporated into one symbol so that the secondary meanings become visible through closer observation.

## Design Decisions

The phoenix was chosen as the central symbol to represent transformation, renewal, and personal expression.

The letter "M" is subtly incorporated into the phoenix's wing, allowing the logo to reference "Meraki" without relying entirely on typography.

The ink droplet is created through negative space within the phoenix. This provides a subtle reference to tattooing while preserving the simplicity of the overall mark.

The black, white, and red palette gives the identity a bold and artistic character. Black provides the primary visual weight, while red acts as an accent that draws attention to key parts of the symbol.

The result is a logo where the individual concepts are not immediately separated from one another, but instead work together as one recognizable mark.

## Final Output

The final identity included:

- Meraki Tattoo Services logo
- Phoenix logo symbol
- Hidden "M" integrated into the phoenix's wing
- Negative-space ink droplet
- Black, white, and red color palette
- Logo application mockups
- Brand presentation materials
